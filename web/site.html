Open Bearings

Everything on one screen.

  • A clear compass. 228° SW, true or magnetic north with the declination, turning the short way past north, with a tick at each point.
  • Where and when. The town you’re in, or how far you are from the nearest one, with the date and time.
  • Six coordinate formats. Decimal degrees, DMS, decimal minutes, UTM, MGRS and Plus Codes. Tap to switch; hold to copy.
  • Elevation in feet and metres at once, from GPS or terrain data, with air pressure where your phone has a barometer.
  • The sun and moon on the dial, with sunrise, sunset and the moon’s phase.
  • Mark a spot, find it again. Where you parked, or camp: its direction on the dial and how far it is.
  • Lock a bearing and see how far you’ve turned from it, plus a bubble level.
  • A map with your heading, north up or following you. Satellite on Apple devices.
  • Honest accuracy. ± for position and heading, a warning near magnets or metal, and speed as you move.
  • Night mode and widgets. All red after dark; widgets for your Home Screen, Lock Screen and desktop.
  • Private and offline. Your location stays on your device; the compass, coordinates, sun and place names work without a connection.

Not a survey instrument.

Phone compasses are thrown off by metal, magnets, cases and cars, and positions and elevations can be wrong. Where it matters, carry a map and a real compass. See the Terms of Use and Privacy.
